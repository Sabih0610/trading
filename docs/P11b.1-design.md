# P11b.1 design — executable data, contracts and costs

Design handoff only; P11b.1 is not implemented or started. P11b.0 delivered an offline reference
shadow engine with explicit rights/causal/durability gates. It supplied no executable quotes,
contract affordability or realistic execution evidence. A future scope needs owner authorization.
No broker sender, EA, demo order or real-money connection is proposed for this data-first step.

## Required evidence boundary

Keep Twelve Data midpoint observations and any later broker bid/ask as distinct sources, identities
and price types. A midpoint cannot be split into fictional sides or used to infer a historical fill.
Acquire bid and ask together with exact source/receipt clocks, quote condition, sequence where
available, session/holiday evidence, transport/age limits and independent gaps/continuity records.
Verify provider permissions for capture, cache, archival, derived analysis and local paper use.

A future adapter should implement the existing ReadOnlyMarketFeed protocol and reuse Bronze/Silver
manifests, immutable provenance, single-writer admission and credential masking. Prefer deterministic
bounded SDK/API reads with measured timeouts and restart behavior. Native MT5 MCP remains limited
to the existing inspection tools; it does not own continuous acquisition or gain an execution tool.
Original MetaQuotes-Demo UNVERIFIED clock profiles cannot be promoted from a stable observed offset;
authoritative exact-server/build/DST semantics plus fresh independent evidence are required.

## Versioned contract and cost facts

Pin source, broker/entity, account environment and instrument separately. Require documentary and
measured tick size/value, lot size/minimum/step, profit/margin currency, pip convention, conversion
quotes, trading sessions/holidays, stop/freeze constraints, commissions, spread observations and
funding/financing treatment. Reconcile XAUUSD tick-value arithmetic. Unknown facts stay unavailable.
The prior minimum-lot/$100 affordability blocker cannot be solved by an invented fractional lot.

Maintain existing independent market/Shariah/legal eligibility decisions. A research contract
simulation is fully funded accounting and does not certify ownership of physical gold/FX or
religious compliance. Current external order authorization remains false. No model may change
risk configuration, enlarge size, move a stop away or access a credential.

## Separately gated local simulation

Only a later approved local-paper scope may combine qualified executable quotes, licensed contract/
cost facts, eligible existing strategies and the deterministic account-risk framework. Preserve
0.5% all-in planned risk, 2% daily net-equity loss, 5% latched peak drawdown, at most 1x funded exposure
and one position under current configuration. Reuse pessimistic next-event accounting and retain
fees/spread/slippage/conversion and gap-risk evidence rather than pretending midpoint fills.

Keep research directional decisions and future simulation accounts in separate journals/exports.
A BUY/SELL research label cannot itself create a position. Local-paper positions/fills, if separately
authorized, must have independently qualified inputs, frozen intent/eligibility IDs, atomic ledger/
risk checkpoints, deterministic reconciliation and no external order capability. Missing quotes,
clock/session/cost/rights/identity or affordability fails closed; AI only vetoes or reduces an
existing eligible deterministic candidate. The dashboard must preserve account/source boundaries.

## Future acceptance design

Test exact bid/ask synchronization and quote staleness, source/credential/profile changes, real
contract arithmetic, price-to-account currency conversion, minimum-lot affordability, pessimistic
costs and gap outcomes, atomic restart/reconciliation, duplicate and out-of-order events, lost locks,
corrupt storage, disk limits and risk latches. Keep no-lookahead and the 2025+ historical holdout
locked; freeze a prospective protocol instead of searching the final set.

Before empirical conclusions, qualify each source/instrument separately, then obtain a separately
approved finite local-paper trial. A later 30+ day study needs its own permission/retention/reliability
and review plan. No current strategy edge or autonomous model promotion is established. Any broker
routing belongs to a new explicitly approved phase and independent safety review, outside P11b.1's
proposed read-only data/contract integration. Existing deferred reviews are not approvals.

## P11b.0.1 technical specification update — 2026-10-24

P11b.1 remains unstarted. Price-only hypotheses now exist for offline research, but cannot enter
an execution/simulation account. No additional broker account, EA or sender was created/connected.
A future implementation should expose typed, immutable, expiring **data** contracts first:

| Data contract | Required authoritative evidence and refusal conditions |
|---|---|
| ExecutableQuote | Exact broker/entity/server/environment/instrument, simultaneous bid/ask, source/receipt UTC, quote flags/sequence, stale/crossed/nonpositive refusal, independent clock profile. Reference midpoints never populate sides. |
| InstrumentContract | Base/quote/profit currencies, tick size/value arithmetic, contract units, lot minimum/step/maximum, account denomination and verified conversion quotes. Unknown units or arithmetic mismatch refuses sizing. |
| CostSchedule | Broker/venue/entity-specific commissions, fee currency/minimum, current spread observations, financing rules and effective dates. No invented spread, commission or zero-cost assumption. |
| SessionPolicy | Broker session calendars, holidays, DST/effective dates, stop/freeze levels and quote/trading permissions. Generic weekday masks cannot replace a verified calendar. |
| SlippageAssumptions | Explicit local-paper hypotheses based on later measured quotes, latency/gaps and size constraints. OHLC cannot establish intrabar path, fill probability or market impact. Use pessimistic bounds; label assumptions, do not call them measured fills. |
| EligibilityEvidence | Applicable jurisdiction/provider/entity access, permitted data uses, instrument/legal review and independent Shariah status. Research approval is neither ownership nor certification. |
| FundedAffordability | Executable ask × base/contract units × minimum lot, verified account-currency conversion plus all costs, bounded by actual cash and at most1x exposure. Existing planned/daily/peak/position risk limits also remain final. |

Each contract needs source ID, version/hash, verified-at/expiry and documentary scope. A clean quote
alone does not qualify legal/Shariah/cost/session/sizing facts. Keep native MCP inspection-only and
source clocks UNVERIFIED until authoritative proof; use deterministic SDK/API timeouts and bounded
recovery tests before relying on acquisition. Do not initialize or change an account for this design.

The prior MetaQuotes-Demo diagnostic metadata was collected2026-10-23; it is not a fresh sizing or
execution qualification. Account denomination was USD. No new broker or price request occurred.

| XAUUSD |100oz/lot ×0.01lot =1oz; step0.01lot |1 × verified executable ask in USD/oz |**NO-GO.** Prior indicative constraint exceeded$100, and a current valid ask is unavailable. These capital thresholds are arithmetic on recorded contract units, not displayed market prices
or a broker recommendation. Any fees/conversion reserve reduce affordable price further. Do not
change1x, invent a smaller lot, assume leverage or substitute a different account/entity to force
feasibility. An alternative unit-sized provider, if ever considered, needs its own legal/rights/
contract/quote review and separate owner authorization; none was opened or connected here.

Only after these read-only contracts pass review may a separate future local-paper scope use
pessimistic deterministic accounting. A research SELL remains a downward prediction and cannot
become a short order. Existing Spot long-only guards, funded risk limits, holdout and no-external-
orders status remain unchanged. ML/LLM comparison or training requires separate sample/rights
and evaluation scope; no automatic retraining, promotion or paid AI was implemented.
