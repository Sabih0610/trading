## Limits

The opt-in Python audit hook blocks external sockets/DNS and the local MT5 endpoint; loopback owned
test servers remain possible. It is an accidental-connection guard, not an operating-system sandbox
against malicious code. Registry setup and Actions acquisition necessarily use the network. No data,
credentials or journals are uploaded or cached. Hosted duration/resource measurements remain unknown.
Synthetic tests cannot verify actual liquidity, partial fills, queue priority, market impact, broker
fees, account permissions, retention rights, eligibility or $100 minimum funded affordability.
