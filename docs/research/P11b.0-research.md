| Engineering choice | Decision |

| Evaluation | Label frozen research signals later using admissible future reference events, not broker profitability. Fixture metrics never become genuine evidence. |

## Measured offline validation and remaining questions

Final suite: 641 passed, 3 optional live deselected, 23 unchanged dependency warnings. Ruff,
338-file formatting, strict MyPy on 133 sources and synthetic paper safety smoke passed. The
owned 128-event M5 replay produced 512 WAIT decisions; a second process suppressed 128 duplicates
without changing decisions. Publication lag in that replay is a constructed 120/121-second
receipt clock, not a provider measurement. No eligible directional strategy or sample exists.

Genuine M5 was deliberately not called because rights remain unresolved. Actual one-credit
headers and real publication/revision behavior therefore remain unverified; tests prove refusal
on changed/missing credit weight. The future collector reconciles official usage and must stop
on quota/cooldown; local reservations alone cannot control another application sharing a key.

Original M1 snapshot and all 16 committed source batches were hash-verified without processing
their numeric prices into this study. Existing records were not deleted. No private display,
retention, AI or model-training permission was inferred from technical connectivity. The current
M1 CLI is also effectively blocked by the new genuine retrieval/cache boundary.
