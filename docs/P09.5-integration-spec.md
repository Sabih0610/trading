## Contract requirements

`VerifiedDemoIdentity` and `DemoOrderProposal` are future specifications, not authorization tokens.
The submit method exists only on a Protocol and has no runtime adapter. A future gateway must
re-read account facts and risk state, cap rather than expand size, and close only owned inventory.
It must never treat possession of this schema as sufficient preflight.

`StructuredMarketAnalyst` returns the existing bounded ArenaAnswer. It cannot create a candidate,
choose an order quantity, move a stop, access broker credentials or enable routing. News is
timestamped, attributed and licensing-checked before becoming a feature; unverified headlines do
not become economic facts. Confidence is not a calibrated win probability.

`OfflineRetrainingPlan` declares a manifest hash, model version, four-thread ceiling, exclusion of
simultaneous local inference and disabled automatic promotion. Existing ML validation and
human-governed registry promotion remain in force. No new model is trained during P09.5.

`MonitoringEvent` is a transport-free record. P11 should emit actionable state changes, shutdowns,
stale-data and reconciliation events with rate limiting and redaction. This phase sends no messages.

## Independent eligibility

Project `shariah_status: approved` is a legacy research policy label. The independent source field
is empty for every instrument. External demo and live eligibility are distinct, false permissions.
Research simulations neither prove physical ownership nor establish legal or Shariah suitability.
Any future real funding requires a separate owner instruction and independent current review.
