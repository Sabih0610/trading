## Delivered components

| Requirement | Implementation and constraint |
|---|---|
| Labels | Every candidate uses its frozen stop, target, and time barrier. Same-bar ambiguity is a loss; right-censored candidates are omitted, not guessed. |
| Validation | Exact availability timestamps; contiguous purged k-fold, expanding past-only folds, combinatorial purged paths, and explicit embargo. |
| Baselines | Class-balanced standardized logistic classification; standardized ridge forecast; recursive ridge; variance persistence; no-regime comparison. |
| Meta-label | Shallow deterministic CPU LightGBM, at most four threads; sigmoid ≥200 and isotonic ≥1,000 held-out rows; Brier/log loss/reliability and drift. |
| Forecasts | Rolling Matern GPR capped at 2,000 rows and 12 features; causal dynamic fair-value filter; HAR-RV and GARCH(1,1). |
| Regime | Seeded diagonal Gaussian HMM limited to 2-4 states plus distribution-shift score. |
| Explanations | Bounded mean absolute TreeSHAP summaries; diagnostic only, never causal evidence. |
| Sizing | Probability strength multiplied by uncertainty discount and clamped to `[0, 1]`; cannot create or enlarge a proposal. |
| Tracking | Local SQLite MLflow metadata/artifacts; registry role requires named human, supports champion/challenger only, and disables automatic deployment. |
