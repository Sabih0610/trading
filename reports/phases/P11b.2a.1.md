(before commit) or immediately after commit, for both entry and exit. Before commit, all account/
ledger/checkpoint changes roll back; retry commits once. After commit, replay finds the complete
transaction and duplicate retries do nothing even though the original caller received no acknowledgement.
Quantity/cash and fill counts reconcile; no double spending or half-position state.
