## Limitations and stop

2021 training is only 2020 Q4. D at 1h needs 168 contiguous prior bars; the 20-trade gate can leave folds without an eligible cell (P11f-3). In this completed batch all nine groups had an eligible training cell in every fold; the two insufficient final verdicts reflect fewer than 30 completed validation trades.
Expanding training and annual evaluation preserve their own unchanged 5% circuit; it can sharply limit completed samples. No risk reset, lower count threshold or later-year substitution was introduced.
The data window is about 4.25 years and these nine experiments are not an executable combined portfolio. Daily-return dependence, finite-sample moment uncertainty and OHLC ordering ambiguity remain limitations.
Five pre-existing audit expected failures remain tracked; none was weakened or silently fixed in this phase. No finer data, filters, grids, costs, metrics or seeds were tried after observations.
F lacks historical books and remains deferred to Stage 5; gold/FX, G and AI/ML remain outside this task.

D SOL has positive expectancy but PF below 1.3, inadequate DSR and failed stress/uncertainty checks; D BTC has negative development expectancy.
