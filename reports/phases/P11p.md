# P11p: Stage 3b swing rotation (BTC, ETH, SOL, gold proxy)

Built, pre-registered and run by Claude (builder and reviewer) while the owner was away, under the owner's
2026-11-06 authorization. Prompt: `prompts/P11p-swing-stage3.md`.

## Verdict: **insufficient evidence** (registered rule)

No development fold had a training cell with the pre-registered minimum of **20 completed trades**:

| Fold | Training window | Best training trade count | Cells ≥ 20 trades |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2022 | 2020-10 → 2021-12 (minus 10-day purge) | 13 | 0 / 27 |
| 2023 | 2020-10 → 2022-12 (minus purge) | 15 | 0 / 27 |

Under the registration, a fold without an eligible cell is insufficient evidence with no substitution. Because the pass
bar requires positive expectancy in 2022, 2023 **and** 2024, **the strategy cannot pass**. The verdict is final for
this registration; nothing was re-tuned.

## Informational only (not a verdict): the frozen 2024 cell

The final selector on data up to 2023-12-31 did find an eligible cell: **trial 9** (pullback to the 20-day SMA, stop
1.5 ATR below the swing low or entry). It was frozen before any 2024 bar loaded, and a rerun reproduced it exactly.

| 2024 (one run) | Value |
|---|---|
| Trades | 21 (1.75 a month) |
| Expectancy | +0.13 R per trade |
| Profit factor | 1.93 |
| Max drawdown | 0.65% |
| Beat random entry (95th percentile) | Yes |
| Beat BTC buy-and-hold on return / drawdown | **No** (BTC +121%, max DD 26%) |

21 trades in a single year is far too small a sample to infer an edge.

## Main finding: frequency

The design trades far less than its 5–15 trades/month target:
- training windows produced only about 13–15 trades each (each included about 6 months of 24-week trend warm-up);
- 2024 produced 1.75 trades a month.

The conjunction of a weekly up-trend, a daily trigger, the 5× cost filter, one position and holds of up to 10 days is
very selective. The 5% peak circuit, which never resets inside a training window, may also have halted training runs
early (not separately recorded for training cells).

## Process record (honest)

Three starts were needed; no outcome was seen before the final run:
1. A crash while **loading** data: XAUUSD market-closed rows have null prices. Fixed: such rows are skipped.
2. A crash in the 2024 **random benchmark**: a random entry had its stop above the decision price. Fixed: such
   entries are cancelled, matching the registered "fill at or below the stop is cancelled" rule. Candidate cells never
   hit this case.
3. A clean run. The frozen 2024 selection matched attempt 2 exactly (trial 9).

Each fix was journaled, committed with the full suite and pushed **before** rerunning. The pre-registration itself never
changed (SHA-256 `ac690876…`).
