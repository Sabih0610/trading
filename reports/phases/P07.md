## What was built

| Area | Result |
|---|---|
| Shared contract | Immutable UTC completed-bar input; ordered replay; approved-asset and per-strategy allowlist checks; entries/exits remain proposals subject to deterministic risk. |
| A — London sweep | DST-aware Europe/London 00:00-07:00 range, 07:00-10:00 low sweep/reclaim, confirmed bullish structure and gap, bounded limit/stop/target, one proposal per weekday. |
| B — XAUT catch-up | Joint positive XAU move and lagging XAUT basis condition, onset-only entry, basis-normalization exit, stop/target, and time stop. |
| C — trend/VWAP | Higher/native bullish structure, ordered rising EMAs, completed VWAP touch/reclaim, bounded stop/target, one proposal per UTC day. |
| Lead-lag | Aligned lag correlations plus a transparent Hayashi-Yoshida overlap-product diagnostic for irregular intervals. |
| Scenario library | Strict versioned crash, rate-pressure, banking-stress, 2024 gold, quiet, official-event, and thin-liquidity definitions; fixed ranges cannot enter 2025+. |
| Stress library | Cost multipliers, extra absolute tick slippage, one-bar delay, one-at-a-time ±20% parameters, seeded Monte Carlo drawdown, and removal of the best 5%. |
| Evidence gate | Path-metadata-only year inventory and deterministic Markdown report; locked observations are never opened. |

Parameter defaults and ranges are frozen in the three Pydantic configuration models. They were not
selected from validation results. Research evidence and limitations are in
`docs/research/P07-research.md`; architectural/data decision D-0021 records why XAUUSD cannot stand
in for executable XAUT.
