## Implementation and decisions

`src/tb/strategies/signals.py` contains frozen UTC daily/weekly input types and pure numeric functions. Known-at filtering precedes visible-history validation; required windows reset on incomplete bars or price-free `HistoryGap` records. Expected-zero closed weekends/holidays are omitted, not labelled missing. Weekly windows require consecutive Monday starts. A signal carries a stable(kind,symbol,bar-open) observation identity; later queries of the same latest bar cannot become new entries.

Trend requires weekly close above its N-week SMA and the SMA above its value M weeks earlier (defaults20/4), with optional daily50/100 confirmation. Breakout excludes its signal bar from the prior high. Pullback requires an explicit up-trend and a touch/cross plus close above completed20/50-day SMA or an actually confirmed swing low. Confirmed pivots are delayed until the later bars finish; pre-gap swing support cannot cross a missing-data barrier.

Each smoothed endpoint uses only its trailing Gaussian window. Double bottoms use two already-confirmed similar troughs and first close above the raw intervening neckline. Bull flags require an upward pole, downward smoothed consolidation and bounded retracement, then first close above raw flag high. The single pattern-family wrapper has fixed double-bottom-before-flag precedence. There are no candlestick or news-entry triggers.

Initial long stops are positive and below entry; optional targets are above entry; holding duration is strictly integer1..10 and never overrides a tighter config. Chandelier stops never lower; if the current executable mark has crossed the raised stop, the wrapper returns a close-only condition instead of loosening it. The engine/RiskManager still controls fills, prices and all permissions. The fixed move hurdle is at least5x two fees+one full quoted spread+two slippages.
