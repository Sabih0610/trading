## Free-plan schedule, cost and finality

- Official `/time_series` documentation lists one credit per symbol. M5 requests use `5min`,
  explicit `UTC`, ascending order and three-bar lookback; no point-rate timestamp assumptions.
- Two symbols × 288 five-minute slots = 576 market credits per 24h. Local application cap is
  600/day, leaving 24 credits within that cap and 200 outside it under the documented 800/day plan.
  The existing 8/minute limit and persisted reservation/cooldown rules still apply.
- Each symbol/close slot is claimed durably once. No repeated same-slot request, automatic retry,
  restart catch-up or cached response becomes a new forward event. Actual account-wide usage can
  differ because of other applications; a later run must reconcile official usage and 429s.
- Genuine M5 requires the actual `api-credits-used` header to equal one. A different/missing weight
  unconfirms the plan and applies a persisted daily cooldown. This behavior is offline-tested;
  the actual M5 header remains unmeasured because rights block the request.
- Schedule at close +120s; accept timely receipt through +180s and processing within 30s of receipt.
  Only the current completed slot with continuous new-run coverage may become forward evidence.
  Lookback rows remain backfill; no finalized-at-close claim or weekend catch-up is made.
- The provider documents typical REST delay of 18–120s. The configured delay is a conservative
  publication policy, not a guarantee of immutable provider candles. Later interval revisions are
  recorded as refused revisions without changing original input/features/decisions.

See [REST docs](https://twelvedata.com/docs),
[publication delays](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5203307-data-delays) and
[credit rules](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5615854-credits).

## Durability and failure evidence

Source open/close, source receipt, admissible availability and decision timestamps remain distinct.
For a close at 10:05 received at 10:07, the decision is at or after 10:07. New-run coverage, receipt
monotonicity, exact origin/instrument/interval, rights and source hashes gate every admitted event.
Old downtime events, future timestamps, backfill, incomplete candles and revised intervals cannot
become current decisions. Decision time also includes measured feature/strategy processing duration.

The consumer holds an OS writer lease. WAL/FULL SQLite stores input, four frozen decisions and a
hash-linked checkpoint in one transaction. Recovery streams and verifies committed evidence, then
reconstructs feature state; it does not assume a missing database is an empty healthy run. The
journal has a 64 MiB configured size cap plus a free-space reserve; refusal is recorded when safe.
This is local process/disk-fault evidence, not a hardware power-loss or 24/7 availability guarantee.
