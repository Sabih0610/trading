## Built

- `BacktestEngine` processes completed UTC bars, delays proposals until the next event, models
  market/limit entries and volume-limited fills, and applies adverse OCO and gap-stop ordering.
- `CostAssumptions` derives fee/spread/slippage from the approved-asset registry and supports
  bounded ×1–×3 stress.
- `RiskManager` is the final quantity authority. It enforces long-only inventory, approved Spot
  assets, sessions, 1% trade risk, 1.0× exposure, cash, daily loss, trade count, kill switch, and
  every available exchange quantity/notional rule before entry and after partial-fill reduction.
- `WalkForwardRunner` provides expanding 2021–2024 chronological folds, separate past/evaluation
  inputs, optional purge, and a locked 2025+ guard that logs explicit authorization before access.
- Baselines cover inactivity, cost-adjusted passive exposure (diagnostic only), prior-day trend, and
  seeded random entries under the same risk checks.
- Metrics cover expectancy, profit factor, drawdown, Sharpe/Sortino with caveats, exposure time,
  trade/trial counts, and a deflated-Sharpe diagnostic.
- Every run writes seven immutable artifacts. The ×1.5 smoke example is in
  `reports/backtests/P06-toy-20261021/`.

## Known limitations

- OHLC bars cannot reveal tick order or queue position; ambiguity is intentionally resolved against
  the strategy. Tick replay is not implemented in P06.
- Registry exchange rules remain nullable until a public metadata synchronizer is built. Every
  available rule is enforced and tests inject realistic constraints, but missing values are not
  guessed.
- The verified project dataset is seven days because the multi-year P02 download needs more disk.
  The smoke report proves accounting mechanics only and cannot validate a strategy.
- Metrics on tiny samples are unstable. Trial count and caveats are emitted, but a benchmark Sharpe
  that reflects all experiments remains the researcher's responsibility.
- Strategy definitions arrive in P07; P06 exposes the shared causal/risk interfaces and baselines.
