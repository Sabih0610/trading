## Implemented changes

- Explicit reference, historical-research and local-simulation permissions; disabled broker-demo
  and live permissions; separate empty independent-approval source. Binance Spot types cannot be
  relabelled as research contracts to bypass approval. Spot guards still refuse XAUUSD/EURUSD.
- Typed contract, bid/ask and currency-conversion facts: contract/lot/tick/pip sizes, minimum
  notional, costs, timestamp/provenance, sessions and downward quantity rounding. New local
  simulations require all metadata; stale/future/mismatched facts reject entry.
- Fully funded contract replay with next-quote fills, stops/targets, gap/slippage, fees, conversion,
  one position, daily frequency and UTC flatness. Partial lot affordability cannot use broker
  buying power. Invalid marks fail closed rather than fabricating a stale-price liquidation.
- Configured 0.5% all-in planned risk, 2% daily net-equity halt, 5% peak drawdown halt, one position,
  and XAUT's tighter 0.25% override. Legacy compiled ceilings of 1% / 3% are upper config bounds;
  active defaults are stricter and models cannot edit or override them.
- Shared liquidation-equity circuit with serializable daily/peak latches. Peak halts survive UTC
  rollover. Spot sizing includes costs and is rechecked at actual fills; gross-exposure sizing
  reserves planned loss. Rejected gaps retain equity rows, and curve equity includes exit costs.
- Instrument-specific chronological coverage assessment. Impossible XAUT discovery is reported
  as insufficient history; fabricated old partition directories cannot make it ready. Existing
  final-test authorization/logging is preserved. The new runner refuses real 2025+ replay outright.
- Reproducible synthetic smoke examples with causal features, risk, local fills, immutable journal
  decisions/outcomes, P&L, rejection/shutdown evidence and checksummed input manifest.
- Typed future dashboard, read-only feed, demo-only proposal/gateway, analyst, retraining and
  monitoring contracts. These are interfaces/specifications; no broker/network/notifier is built.

## Remaining limits

There is no authentic attached history, empirical edge, market-trained model, independent Shariah
certification, broker-specific verified contract metadata or verified demo identity. Legacy Spot
toy replay still accepts nullable exchange filters and is unsuitable for routing. New contract
facts are complete fixture assumptions with explicit synthetic provenance, not broker settings.
Coverage assessment is metadata only; it does not prove continuity, quality or sufficient labels.

This is bounded offline replay, not the continuous live-feed/paper runtime. State serialization is
provided, but restart reconciliation, atomic runner persistence, terminal connectivity, source
licensing, notifications and 24h/30+ day soaks remain future work. Independent P06/P09 reviews remain
deferred and P09.5 is queued. No real-money gateway is present.
