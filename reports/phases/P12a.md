## Coverage notes for S2

- Only about 43% of filings carry a standard debt tag. Many are small companies with no debt, but S2 should
  require `debt_reported` (or a total-liabilities cross-check) before a company can pass.
- About 60–65% of annual filings have no interest-income tag, so they use the conservative 5.5% estimate.
  This biases toward exclusion.
