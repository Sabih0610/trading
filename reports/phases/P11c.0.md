## Limits and readiness

EURUSD: owned workflow ready; genuine source-data evaluation blocked without a
qualified dataset. XAUUSD: independently the same status; no implicit gold access.
Executable broker bid/ask, contracts, liquidity, fees, fill evidence and genuine
account P&L remain unavailable. Synthetic side-price and paper scenario results
are SOFTWARE TEST ONLY. Midpoint/bid-only directional outcomes cannot establish
execution performance, Sharpe, account return or realistic monetary profitability.

MVP limits are 64 MiB/50,000 input rows per registered local shard and 1,000-row
parse/normalized chunks; at most 50,000 normalized rows are materialized for future
labels. It does not automatically aggregate multi-year shards or convert arbitrary
provider schemas. Future converters must document historical availability rather
than invent latency. Insufficient coverage/count is a valid research outcome.

Rights verification is a human documentary decision with software enforcement;
local metadata is not a signature against an owner rewriting every hash. Inventory
covers declared workflow artifacts, including interrupted writes, and explicitly
reports absent categories. Arbitrary manual copies, unrelated old journals and
filesystem-level deletion prevention are outside its discovery/enforcement scope.
No actual disposal occurs. Open output files through the gated commands/view while
rights remain current; external file viewers cannot enforce application policy.
