## Frozen definitions and parameter versions

`price-only-strategies-1.0.0` uses `shared-price-only-2.0.0`; config2.0.0 contains research
parameters1.0.0. Shared indicator periods are fast12/slow26/channel20, ATR14. EMA uses first-close
seed and alpha2/(n+1). Both wait for52 contiguous completed observations; configuration cannot
omit required lookback. No reserved historical observations or empirical parameter search was used.

| Hypothesis | BUY/SELL condition | WAIT / suppression |
|---|---|---|
| EMA trend | First eligible non-neutral EMA regime after warm-up, then regime transitions; fast above slow predicts UP, below predicts DOWN. | Separation<=0.1ATR is neutral. Warm-up, neutral, filters and unchanged active direction yield WAIT. |
| Price channel | Completed close above prior20 highs +0.1ATR predicts UP; below prior20 lows -0.1ATR predicts DOWN. Current candle excluded from both thresholds. | Inside/touch/wick-only returns WAIT; optional1-3 completed-close confirmations, default1. Repeated active same-side breakouts suppressed until neutral/opposite. |

Both require nonzero ATR, ATR/close within0..0.02 and current true range<=4 times prior ATR.
Filtered transitions are consumed, so a later quieter candle cannot recreate a missed setup.
Gaps reset feature/strategy continuity and warm-up. EURUSD/XAUUSD state is separate. Decisions
occur at/after actual receipt plus processing, never at candle close. Completed/source/receipt/
coverage/rights validation precedes features. Duplicates/revisions never rewrite old decisions.

The original do-nothing WAIT control and London sweep/VWAP/XAUT catch-up definitions remain
unchanged; the latter retain all original mapping/session/volume/HTF/basis/cost blockers.
A research SELL is a downward hypothesis, not a position or permission to sell an unowned asset.
Funded account risk is still unavailable for midpoint-only decisions; no AI/risk override exists.

## Frozen future outcomes and baselines

`future-reference-1.0.0` is pinned in journal metadata/config before the first event. Horizons are
1/3/6 full M5 intervals starting at the first opening>=decision time; flat threshold0bps. Each
label requires all horizon bars, contiguous, same-source and committed, with receipts<=label time.
The change is first future open to final future close. No input-close backdating or invented fill.
WAIT controls are excluded. Missing windows are not losses, and not-yet-available windows remain
pending. Labels append without changing original signal, configuration, timestamps or features.

The final owned study contains94 available horizon labels for34 scenario-specific directional
records. There are8 missing outcome windows and0 pending windows across the final scenario groups;
these are separate from incorrect/flat outcomes (12 descriptive labels). Every group is
marked `synthetic_only_no_statistical_inference`; counts are far too small and deliberately
constructed to establish genuine skill. The false-breakout/spike examples include incorrect/flat
outcomes. No combined accuracy or trading win rate is presented as empirical evidence.

The always-WAIT control has zero scored directional outcomes. Always-UP and the sign of the
available prior price move are scored on the same windows as each candidate; a neutral prior move
abstains. Counts are reported per symbol/strategy/horizon. Greedy non-overlap flags and separate
all/non-overlapping counts prevent treating overlapping windows as independent trials. Horizons,
instruments and constructed scenarios are not independent, and no pooled statistical significance
or trading win rate is claimed. Minimum30 non-overlapping observations is a descriptive refusal
threshold, not a proof of power; every owned result remains synthetic-only with no market inference.

minimum/step, denomination/conversion, commissions/spreads, sessions/holidays, stop/freeze levels,
slippage limitations, legal/provider eligibility and independent Shariah status. It is design only.
A$100 account remains **NO-GO** for a qualified minimum fully funded trade in either instrument:
prior diagnostic minima were1000EUR and1oz; current valid executable/cost/legal facts are missing.
The arithmetic affordability bounds would be ask<=0.10USD/EUR and<=$100/oz before costs. These are
capital thresholds, not market prices; earlier indicative diagnostics exceeded capital and cannot
become current sizing approval. No1x/risk change, invented lot or additional broker account.

The [conditional genuine procedure](../../docs/P11b.0.1-supervised-trial-plan.md) requires provider
rights and a separate owner approval before any trial. A fresh two-hour exercise cannot satisfy
52 M5 bars (260 minutes) and must remain warm-up WAIT. Warm-up plus6 future intervals needs at
least290 source minutes before margins; a later meaningful signal trial requires a separately
scoped longer finite process/continuity/retention design. This phase did not widen120-minute
bounds, use unseen historical warm-up, install24/7 supervision, train/promote models or start P11b.1.
