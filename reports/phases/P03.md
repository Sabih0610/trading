# P03 phase report — Calendar and news

P03 delivers terms-aware, multi-source Bronze calendar/news ingestion, reconciliation, deduplication, and coverage reporting. Official schedules cover every target month; four independent RSS streams produced a live headline sample, while GDELT history remains a resumable background job waiting for provider cooldown.

## Summary

The calendar run fetched official BLS yearly schedules, the ECB monetary-policy webcast history, and recent/global Finance Calendar records. After deterministic reconciliation, it produced 522 high-impact events across all 73 UTC months from October 2020 through October 2026: BLS 384, ECB 47, Finance Calendar 103. Official source times win conflicts while aggregator values can fill nullable actual/forecast/previous fields.

The bounded live news run produced 56 unique headlines for 2026-10-20 from CoinDesk (13), Cointelegraph (26), Decrypt (16), and ECB (1). It stored headline/link metadata only. GDELT returned HTTP 429 after research requests; retries exhausted cleanly, the independent RSS sources continued, and the six-year GDELT command is documented for cooldown/resume.

Every payload is retained content-addressed under `_raw`; normalized Parquet snapshots are immutable, manifested, cached, and content/version sensitive. The shared Bronze validator passed 125 files and 12,673,099 rows with zero issues after P03.

## Known limitations

- The GDELT six-year headline archive is not complete. The public DOC service returned HTTP 429 after research probes; the resumable daily-window job is recorded as waiting on provider cooldown.
- Daily GDELT article lists cap at 250. Saturated days are warned and cannot be described as exhaustive without narrower follow-up queries.
- Finance Calendar reaches only about 12 months backward; it contributes recent global breadth, not historical authority.
- Historical calendar coverage is high-impact US BLS plus ECB monetary-policy events. Older BEA and broader non-US indicator history remain explicit source gaps rather than inferred rows.
- Fair Economy live storage, Nasdaq automation, and live FRED remain terms-gated.

## How to run

```powershell
Set-Location -LiteralPath "E:\trading bot"
pwsh -NoProfile -File .\scripts\download-calendar.ps1 -Sources bls,ecb,finance_calendar -From 2020-10-01 -To 2026-10-21 -Resume
pwsh -NoProfile -File .\scripts\download-news.ps1 -Sources gdelt,federal_reserve,ecb,coindesk,cointelegraph,decrypt -From 2026-10-20 -To 2026-10-20 -Resume
```

After GDELT cooldown, run the historical job:

```powershell
pwsh -NoProfile -File .\scripts\download-news.ps1 -Sources gdelt -From 2020-10-01 -To 2026-10-21 -Resume
```
