Scope/identity rechecks, token invalidation and account/symbol changes stop acquisition. Reconnect requires full verification and empty side state.

## Quote normalization and financial completeness

Prices use Decimal/raw value divided by 100000, independently of display digits. Native volume/lot fields divide by 100 into units. Preserve raw field values/presence, original timestamp representation and provider/account/instrument/metadata/rights identity. No smaller fractional broker functionality or source sequence is invented.

Bid and ask retain separate source and actual wire-receipt timestamps, including queued messages. Partial updates never renew the other side; gaps expire it. Require positive, un-crossed, precision-aligned sides, non-future source clocks, no receipt/source regression and at most five-second age for both sides. Missing/wrong-unit/stale/future clocks refuse; no timezone offset is applied. Millisecond decoding is an explicit fixture/protocol contract; genuine independent UTC/unit evidence remains an unverified preflight condition. Quotes are indicative dealing prices, not observed/guaranteed fills.

Sessions require explicit intervals/timezone/holiday presence. Nonempty holiday sets currently require interpretation review and fail closed. Precision digits are not a verified economic tick size/value. Reported tick cash values must reconcile with verified tick size × lot units in the stated currency; contradictions refuse.

Product/profit currency, economic tick size/value, freeze levels and complete costs remain unknown unless explicitly evidenced. Raw commission/swap/admin fields are retained when present, without guessed totals or zero-fee assumptions. Cost evidence must bind account/contract/currency/basis/expiry. Legal and independent Shariah eligibility remain separate and unavailable; swap-free labels cannot establish either.
