## Assessment (before any outcome is seen)

- On the conventional 3-year window, contrarian "crowd is short" signals happen in **one cluster (2022)**.
  Consecutive weeks in one episode are not independent, so 20+ "trades" there would still be about
  one bet.
- The 2024 validation year has **0–6** contrarian signal weeks, too few for a one-time pass/fail check.
- The 26-week window fires more often and more evenly. It is the only version with a usable sample, but it is
  still thin.

**Decision:** the 18-trial G1–G4 design is **not registered as written**. A registration would most likely
produce "insufficient evidence" again and spend deflated-Sharpe budget. Better uses of this data:
1. **As a filter**, not a trigger: block or allow gold entries from other strategies when the crowd is
   extremely long or short (one pre-registered on/off rule, very few extra trials).
2. **Longer history**: CFTC data goes back to 1986 (legacy). A study over 2006–2024 would need a longer
   rights-checked gold price series than we hold (ours starts 2020). That is a free-data search for later.

Both are proposals for the owner; nothing has been tested.
