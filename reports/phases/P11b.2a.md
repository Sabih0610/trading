## Deterministic execution and position lifecycle

Frozen completed research observation → original available decision → eligibility/shared risk →
local owned intention → subsequent admissible side observations → hypothetical all-or-none fill or refusal.
IDs derive from package/account/decision/input content, not UUIDs or wall-clock time. Inputs have
stable dedup IDs; changed payload under a frozen ID refuses. Strategy/source/rights versions are pinned.
Owned fixture processing is deterministic even across independent output roots.

BUY may request a fully funded long; SELL can close an owned long only, and cancels a pending long
entry. SELL without inventory is research-only and rejected for execution. WAIT creates no intention.
The source ResearchDecision retains execution_eligible=false; paper eligibility is a separate owned mapping.

Orders have **pending, rejected, expired, filled and cancelled** states. Terminal states cannot be
rewritten. One pending entry reserves capital; another rejected signal cannot release that reservation
or spend it. Entry eligibility, opening, holding/management and closing have journal reasons inspectable
in the UI. All-or-none is an explicit limitation; no partial-fill claim about real markets.

Entry: actual owned ASK plus configured adverse ticks, explicit fee, full principal debit. Both sides'
source and receipt times must be strictly after the intention. Prior midpoints, stale initial/closed
quotes or fresh receipts of pre-decision source facts cannot fill. Recheck cash/grid/session/freshness,
trade/position/exposure/risk and exact approved quantity at fill. Changed quantity, price cap or reserved
funds failure rejects; no automatic enlargement or unreviewed resize.

Exit: owned BID minus ticks and fee. Standing stop/target/risk/day protection is established on entry;
only later eligible quotes can trigger it. Explicit SELL exit intentions also require later quotes.
Stop gaps use the observed adverse quote, never a guaranteed stop price. Take-profit triggers receive
no favorable gap improvement (market-trigger semantics, not a guaranteed limit fill). Missed-fill flags
remain pending; entries/signal exits expire after 30 seconds, protection remains until inventory closes.
End-of-day instructions cancel entries and require a later valid exit quote. Missing sessions/marks or
expired contract facts cannot invent flatness or proceeds. Unresolved inventory is reported, not hidden.

## Contracts, costs, accounts and risk

Owned EURUSD-style contract: 100,000 units/lot, 0.01 minimum/step, tick 0.00001, pip 0.0001.
Owned gold-style contract: 100 units/lot, 0.01 minimum/step, tick/pip 0.1. Both have explicit positive
fees (default 1 bp per side) and one adverse tick per fill. These are versioned **invented test facts**,
not real broker specifications, physical ownership or financial/religious approval. Missing commission,
contradictory monetary tick arithmetic, wrong units/currency or genuine evidence mode refuses.

Account A starts at $100, account B at $10,000, separately in each scenario. All 14 account-A studies
remain at $100 with zero fills/positions. Actual admissible BUY examples cannot fund the illustrative
minimum full notional; the correct result is refusal. Fifteen rejected account-A intentions also include
research-only/missing-quote cases. Account B has four rejected intentions in the complete demonstration.
No lot minimum, risk policy or borrowing rule was changed to create entries.

Preserve **0.5% all-in planned risk, 2% daily net-equity halt, 5% peak drawdown, maximum 1x exposure,
one position, trade counts and stricter overrides**. Shared effective limits also check ongoing marked
exposure. Daily/peak latches persist across restart. Shared daily rollover can clear the daily latch;
peak halts survive rollover. New entries halt; valid exits of owned inventory remain available. Unknown
exit marks make equity/unrealized P&L unavailable and cannot support new sizing.

Golden independent example: 1,000 units enter at 1.10003, fee 0.110003; cash becomes 8899.859997.
Exit-side mark 1.09999 after fee gives equity 9999.739998 and unrealized P&L -0.260002.
An exit at 1.10099 with fee 0.110099 yields final cash 10000.739898, total fees 0.220102 and realized
P&L 0.739898. These literal oracles and generated algebra check the implementation independently.
Fees/slippage variants change only explicit costs, not strategy parameters. No upstream library was
run as an accounting oracle or adopted as authority.

Cash equals initial cash plus committed fill cash deltas. Equity minus initial capital equals realized
plus unrealized P&L whenever marks exist. Fees are charged once; spread and adverse adjustment are
already in fill prices and are not charged again. Quoted-spread diagnostics describe quotes at fills;
"adverse slippage" includes foregone target-gap improvement, not a measured broker statistic.

## Atomic journal, recovery and integrity

Same local SQLite database as DecisionLedger; FULL-sync atomic transaction includes the raw input ID,
all intention/fill/rejection/exit/fee/valuation/risk effects, cash/positions/reservations/anchors, and
append-only checkpoint hash chain. OS lease prevents competing writers across processes. Failed writers
are poisoned and must reopen. Restart replays committed inputs and compares reconstructed state/effects,
manifest/source/risk pins, ledger hashes and chain. Recovery itself is append-only, deterministic and
idempotent for a verified checkpoint. No automatic clearing, repair or silent migration.

Fault tests interrupt before intention commit, after intention before fill, during fill/equity, exit/risk
and ledger/checkpoint writes, and during recovery-journal writing. Rollback leaves no partial cash or
fill. A committed fill survives missing dashboard export. Replay cannot duplicate local fills or P&L;
this is **not an exactly-once external brokerage claim**. Corruption, source/version switch, disk capacity,
missing initialization, closed lease and second-writer attempts refuse safely. Bounds: 2,048 default input
events/128 MiB per study, size/event limits and minimum free space.
