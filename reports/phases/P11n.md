## Implemented contract

Daily bars use left-closed UTC days; weekly bars open Monday00:00 UTC. Bars retain
observed, expected, observed-expected and unexpected minute counts. Completeness
requires at least95% expected coverage, an ended window entirely inside admitted
history, and no calendar conflict. Missing prices stay null; expected-zero closed
periods are metadata, never fabricated complete OHLC. Gap knowledge becomes visible
at the window's exclusive end; missing expected-open windows reset feature history.

The shared FeatureEngine has an opt-in, separately bounded swing stream. Fixed
SMA/EMA20/50/100/200 and slopes, Wilder ATR14, prior-bar Donchian20/55, sample RV20
and strict three-left/three-later confirmed swings are versioned in each manifest.
Native intraday calculations and old medallion generations are unchanged. Long
weekly warm-ups may remain null; no earlier price series is borrowed to seed them.
The O/P bridge preserves HistoryGap/incomplete-bar barriers and the fixed lag.

Private immutable day-bound1m paths are available only for fill ordering after that
day ends, with current rights, source identities, bounds and checksums rechecked.
Other-day requests refuse before IO. Minute OHLC leaves within-minute stop/target
order ambiguous; P must pre-register that convention and sensitivity. P must also
filter ended/available windows against its cutoff, including weekly windows that
cross a year-folder boundary. Generation complete means publication completed;
it never asserts that every contained bar is complete.

New immutable outputs and pointers use only the separate swing-v1 namespace.
Data Health reads JSON coverage metadata; no strategy or chart analysis is added.
